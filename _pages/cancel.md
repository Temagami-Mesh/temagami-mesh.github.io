## Project Cancellation

This project has failed to pass the feasibility assessment stage and has been cancelled. For the benefit of others who might be considering a similar project elsewhere, a list of reasons why this project is not feasible, and potential workarounds are described below:

## Technical Performance Issues

Most of our test nodes have gone offline due to lack of solar power. The 5-8W panels are fine for summer use but not for northern winters. The only nodes still functioning have ample and direct sunlight exposure. There are also unresolved issues with cold weather trickle charging of lithium batteries that we have not been able to verify.

Bigger panels, more vegetation removal, and a switch to alternative battery technologies could solve these issues. 

## Lack of Volunteer Support

We have tried to incorporate as a not-for-profit corporation that could own permits and property. Ultimately we were unable to recruit enough active volunteers to continue and so the corporation has been dissolved. While there is substantial public interest in the project, that interest was simply not translating into active volunteer support. 

Most people see communications technology as something others must build for them and that they pay a fee for. They have trouble understanding that with mesh networks they are responsible for purchasing and installing the equipment and acting as part of the network. 

## Impossible Permit Situation

Over the course of this study we made numerous requests for information regarding what permits would be required to do this project and what they would cost. Ultimately the government was unable to produce any such information or any suitable application process for this project. They are treating our node sites as cellular tower sites, which they are not. They would understand this if they read the project information we sent them but they refuse to read it or have failed to comprehend it. 

A new permit application process needs to be developed for mesh networking projects. Until that happens, expect to have bureaucrats either send you application forms that are impossible to fill out for your project. Most of them just refuse to meet or return calls so they don't have to deal with the fact that they are unable to process our application.

## Economically Depressed Region

This problem was carried out in a region that is generally considered uninvestable and where often the only source of income is government handouts and subsidies. Most people viewed this project as an economic development project and were looking for jobs, free equipment, fees for dubious services, etc. There were very few people who were genuinely interested in solving the problems this project was trying to solve.
