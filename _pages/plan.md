## Project Cancellation

This project has failed to pass the feasibility assessment stage and has been cancelled. For the benefit of others who might be considering a similar project elsewhere, a list of reasons why this project is not feasible are below:

## Technical Performance Issues

Most of our test nodes have gone offline due to lack of solar power. The 5-8W panels are fine for summer use but not for the winter. The only nodes still functioning have ample direct sunlight exposure. There are also unresolved issues with cold weather charging of lithium batteries. 

Bigger panels, more vegetation removal, and a switch to alternative battery technologies could solve these issues. 

## Lack of Volunteer Support

We have tried to incorporate as a not-for-profit corporation that could own permits and property. Ultimately we were unable to recruit enough active volunteers to continue and so the corporation has been dissolved. While there is substantial public interest in the project, that interest was simply not translating into active volunteer support. This project is only viable as a not-for-profit corporation, it is not viable as a profit making subscription service.

## Failed Attempts to Obtain Permitting Information

We made numerous requests for information regarding what permits would be required to operate on crown land and what they would cost. Ultimately the MNR was unable to produce any such information or a suitable application process for this project. They are treating our node sites as cellular tower sites, which they are not. They would understand this if they read the project information we sent them but they refuse to read it or have failed to comprehend it. 

We tried to send a delegation to the Municipality of Temagami but were denied due to the elections. 

## Other Human Factors

Most people view this project as an economic development project and are looking for jobs, equipment handouts, and to extract money from the project's donors in the form of permits, penalties and fees. There were very few people who were genuinely interested in solving the problems this project was trying to solve.
