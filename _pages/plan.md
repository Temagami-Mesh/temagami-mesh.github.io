## Project Cancellation ##

This project failed to pass the feasibility assessment stage and has been cancelled. While we know the technology will work and has broad public support, there have been too many impediments to organizing and obtaining permits to justify proceeding any further. 

## Failed Attempt to Organize

In the fall of 2026 we explored the possibility of incorporating as a not-for-profit corporation that could own permits and property. Ultimately we were unable to recruit the required minimum number of directors. 

## Failed Attempts to Obtain Permitting Information

We also made numerous requests for information regarding what permits would be required to operate on crown land and what they would cost. Ultimately the government was unable to produce any such information or a suitable application process for this project.
